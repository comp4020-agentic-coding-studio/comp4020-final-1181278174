# Crit 10 — Fly by instruments

## The breakthrough

The breakthrough was saying that I did not know what the agent had built.
On the night before the crit, the agent explained its plan for this week, and
I did not understand it. A shorter version, with five example lines from the
log, made the decision clear, and I chose to log who asked. The agent then built it and reported
back, and I did not understand the report either. That was when I said that
since the final project started I had let the agent work on its own, and that
I did not know what it, or the agent in the earlier weeks, had built. The
agent stopped building. It went through the three weeks one small piece at a
time, in plain words, and gave me something to try each time.

## What it changed

In crit 9 I wrote that I want to understand a decision before I approve it.
This week showed me that understanding the decisions is not enough if I do not
know what was built around them. A report from the agent says what it did, but
I only know it once I have seen it myself. The logs added this week are one way
to see it: they show what the app did, without anyone's summary.

From now on I want to try each step myself before the agent starts the next
one, and to ask for an example when an explanation does not make sense to me.
