# Crit 9 — All at once

## The breakthrough

The breakthrough was using the app myself on two devices. Until then,
everything I knew about it came from the agent's reports and from tests that
passed. I opened the projector page on my laptop and scanned its QR code with
my phone. The phone opened the session. On the laptop I thought the projector
page was the app, but it has no box for asking and it had no link to the page
that has one. All the tests passed while that was true, so I had a link added.
Then I pressed "Me too" on my phone, and the question moved to the top of the
list on my laptop. That was the first time I understood what the app does when
several people use it at once.

## What it changed

This crit asked me to make one decision about that and to say why. The agent
gave me two options and recommended one. I picked the other without
understanding either of them, and the plan recorded my choice with a reason the
agent had written. I only noticed when I was asked to explain the decision and
could not. After I tried the app I kept the decision, because anyone can see
straight away which question the class wants most, and I replaced the reason
with that one.

I want to understand a decision before I approve it. When I pick an option
from a list, it is still my decision, and I am the person who has to explain
it at the crit. Next time I am offered a choice I do not understand, I will ask
for an explanation or try the thing first, and answer after that.
